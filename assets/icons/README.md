# Footer icons

`academia.svg`, `github.svg`, `googlescholar.svg`, `linkedin.svg`, `orcid.svg`,
`publons.svg`, `researchgate.svg`, and `scopus.svg` come from
[Simple Icons v11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons),
distributed under CC0-1.0 (see `LICENSE-simple-icons.md`). Their fills are set
for the dark footer. Brand names and logos remain subject to their owners'
trademark policies.

`email.svg` is a custom envelope drawing for this site.

`lattes.svg` and `webofscience.svg` are extracted from the `lattes` and
`clarivate` glyphs in [Academicons v1.9.4](https://github.com/jpswalsh/academicons/tree/v1.9.4),
by James Walsh and Katja Bercic, licensed under SIL OFL 1.1 (see `LICENSE-academicons.txt`).
They are standalone SVG paths with a monochrome fill for the dark footer.
